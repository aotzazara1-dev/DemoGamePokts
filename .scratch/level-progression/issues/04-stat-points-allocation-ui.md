Status: ready
Blocked by: 03

## Objective
Implement an interactive Stat Points allocation interface allowing players to distribute earned Stat Points to ATK, DEF, INT, and AGI for both their Hero avatar and captured Beasts.

3. Support hotkey `C` for Hero Character Status & Stat Allocation.
4. Add Level Up celebration banner in Overworld when leveling up.

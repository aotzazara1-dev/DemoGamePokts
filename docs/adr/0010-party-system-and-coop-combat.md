# Party Train Navigation and Cooperative Combat Jamming

We adopted the classic TS Online party system where up to five Heroes form a Party, with non-leader members automatically tethered behind the Party Leader across the Overworld. When an encounter triggers, all members enter the same Battle Instance with their Active Beasts occupying the 2x5 Formation Grid. Additionally, non-partied players in the Overworld can Jam into ongoing combat to reinforce allies.

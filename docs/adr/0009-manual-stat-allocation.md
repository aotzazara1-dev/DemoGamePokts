# Manual Stat Point Allocation Upon Level Up

We chose manual Stat Point distribution upon level up rather than automatic stat growth curves. Players freely allocate points across HP, SP, ATK, DEF, INT, and AGI, enabling build diversity (e.g. Pure AGI, Pure INT, Tank DEF) and critical speed-tuning required to fulfill Combo conditions (|AGI difference| <= 15) with party members and Beasts.
